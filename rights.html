Your Rights

In India, ragging is a crime. Colleges must stop it and must act when someone complains.

You have the right to:

  • Be treated with respect and dignity
  • Report ragging without fear
  • Report it even if you went along with it because you were scared
  • Go straight to the police and file an FIR
  • Ask for your name to be kept private
  • Get medical help and counselling

What happens to people who rag?

They can be suspended from classes, barred from exams, removed from the hostel, expelled from the college and face a police case.

This page is general information, not legal advice. Read the full rules at antiragging.in.
