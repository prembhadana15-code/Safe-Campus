Helplines

These calls are free. In danger right now? Call 112 first.

1800-180-5522 - National Anti-Ragging Helpline (open 24 hours)
112 - Emergency (police, ambulance)
100 - Police
1098 - Childline (for anyone under 18)
181 - Women Helpline
14416 - Tele-MANAS (mental health support)

You can also tell your college's Anti-Ragging Committee or Squad, your warden or your principal. Email the national helpline at helpline@antiragging.in.

Numbers can change, so check antiragging.in for the latest.
