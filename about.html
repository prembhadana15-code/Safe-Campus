About

Stop Ragging is a simple website where any student, parent or friend can report ragging and find help.

What this site does

  • Lets you report ragging, with or without your name
  • Explains your rights
  • Lists helplines you can call

What it does not do

It is not an emergency service and it does not give legal advice. If someone is in danger, call 112.
